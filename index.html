TensorNets

Four units on how the TensorNets nets are made. Each unit has a write-up (HTML, PDF, Markdown) and a Wolfram notebook with the same section numbers: a toolkit cell, then one section per idea, each with a worked example by hand and a check that the kernel returns. Units 1–3 follow one paper each; Unit 4 builds our own net from them.

Open a notebook in Wolfram, choose Evaluation ▸ Quit Kernel ▸ Local, and evaluate it from top to bottom. The notebooks are saved with their outputs, so they can also be read without running.

Design: DESIGN-tensornets.pdf · Glossary · Units

Unit 1 · Reading a Sequence, Writing a Sequence

Deep LSTMs as encoder and decoder (Sutskever, Vinyals and Le, 2014): the LSTM cell by hand, the thought vector, reversing the source, the training recipe, beam search, the 384M parameter count.

Read · PDF · Markdown · Notebook unit1_seq2seq.nb

Unit 2 · Mathematics as Data

Lample and Charton (2019): prefix notation, counting and uniform sampling of trees, FWD/BWD/IBP integration data, verified answers, and a correction to their Equation 2.

Read · PDF · Markdown · Notebook unit2_symbolic_data.nb

Unit 3 · Attention, Part by Part

Vaswani et al. (2017): every part of the transformer from plain arrays and from primitive Wolfram layers, checked against each other.

Read · PDF · Markdown · Notebook unit3_transformer.nb

Unit 4 · Building Our Own Net

A transformer assembled from Units 1–3 that expands ⨂/⨁ expressions, decoded with beam search and the prefix grammar, every answer checked by an exact oracle.

Read · PDF · Markdown · Notebook unit4_build_your_own.nb
